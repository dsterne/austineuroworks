Services

What we actually do, in detail

Ten services, explained the way we'd explain them in the bay — not SEO filler. All makes and models welcome, every job backed by a 2-year / 24,000-mile nationwide warranty.

01

Engine Repair & Replacement

Engine work starts with a real diagnosis, not a parts cannon. We scope compression, oil consumption, timing components, and sensor data before we quote anything, so you're not paying to "try" a fix. On the engines that need it, including many European ones, we deal with timing chain stretch, VANOS/variable valve issues, and oil leak sources (valve cover gaskets, oil filter housing gaskets) that generic shops often misdiagnose. Full replacements are sourced OEM or OEM-equivalent, your call on budget.

02

Brake Repair & Replacement

Pads, rotors, calipers, brake lines, and the master cylinder behind all of it. We measure rotor thickness and pad wear rather than eyeballing it, and we'll tell you honestly if you've got another 5,000 miles left instead of selling you a brake job today. Squealing, grinding, soft pedal, pulling to one side — all diagnosed and quoted before any work starts.

03

Oil Change

Right viscosity and spec for your engine — Many manufacturers (VW/Audi and Mercedes included) have specific approval ratings that generic quick-lube shops often skip. We also do a full fluid and filter check while the car's up, so an oil change doubles as a quick health check on belts, hoses, and leaks.

04

Electrical Diagnostics

Dash warning lights, intermittent electrical gremlins, dead modules, battery drain issues — this is where the real diagnostic work separates shops from parts-swappers. We run OEM-level scan tools (not just a generic code reader) to actually trace the fault instead of clearing the code and hoping it doesn't come back.

05

Suspension & Steering Repair

Struts, shocks, control arms, bushings, tie rods, and alignment. If your car pulls, bounces, clunks over bumps, or wanders at highway speed, this is almost always the cause. We also handle the self-leveling and adaptive suspension systems found on higher-trim luxury models, which most general shops won't touch.

06

Battery Replacement

We load-test before we recommend a replacement — a weak battery isn't always a dead battery. On newer vehicles, battery replacement often requires registering the new battery with the car's electrical system so the charging profile stays correct; we handle that coding as part of the job, not as an upsell.

07

Transmission Repair & Replacement

From fluid service and filter changes to full rebuilds, on both automatic and manual transmissions. Dual-clutch and multi-speed automatic transmissions need manufacturer-specific fluid and service intervals — using the wrong fluid is one of the most common ways these get ruined by shops that don't specialize.

08

Tire Rotation

Front-to-back rotation on a regular interval evens out tread wear and extends tire life meaningfully — a five-minute job that saves you a full tire's worth of money over time. We'll flag uneven wear patterns that point to alignment or suspension issues while we're under there.

09

Belts & Hoses Replacement

Serpentine belts, timing belts (on the engines that use them instead of chains), and coolant hoses age and crack from heat cycling even if they look fine from the top. A snapped timing belt can take an engine with it, so we service these on manufacturer interval, not just when they visibly fail.

10

Fuel System Repair

Fuel pumps, injectors, fuel lines, and the rough-running, hard-starting, or poor-mileage symptoms they cause. Direct-injection engines common on newer makes carry carbon buildup on intake valves that port-injection engines don't — we check for and address that as part of fuel system work when it's the actual cause.

Not sure which one you need?

Tell us what the car's doing and we'll point you in the right direction — no appointment required to ask.
