Why AEW

We work on these cars because we like them, not because we have to.

Most shops treat a European car like a problem to avoid. We built this one around being good at it.

AEW started as a family operation and still runs like one — small enough that the person who talks to you about your car is usually the person who worked on it. Over the years that's meant building real depth on Audi, BMW, Mercedes-Benz, and VW, without losing the ability to handle whatever everyday domestic or Asian make rolls in behind it.

That mix is on purpose. If you drive something German, you don't want a generalist guessing at it. If you drive a Honda, you don't want to pay European shop labor rates for an oil change. We do both, correctly, under one roof in Leander.
